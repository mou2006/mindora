ABOUT MINDSENTINEL

Understand Your Emotional Wellbeing

MindSentinel is an academic project prototype that demonstrates how artificial intelligence and machine learning can support emotional wellbeing monitoring.

The platform combines mood tracking, text-based emotion detection, wellbeing assessment, distress screening indicators, and supportive AI interaction.
